A field of its own, not a label.

  h2a  a person asked an agent
  a2h  an agent needs a person: a decision, an approval, a credential
  a2a  one agent handed work to another
  h2h  two people

It answers the question that matters most in a backlog worked by people and agents
together: WHAT IS WAITING ON ME? An a2h ticket is blocking a machine that would
otherwise still be working, and neither status nor priority can say that.
